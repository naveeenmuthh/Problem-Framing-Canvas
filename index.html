EdTech AI in Education K-12 UX Research
01 — Key insights (top 5)
Insight 01
Zoom is present but structurally misaligned
Zoom has ~90% meeting penetration in K-12 and ~85% in higher ed — yet it lacks attendance tracking, gradebooks, LMS integration, and parent communication. It's a business tool coerced into a classroom.
Insight 02
The AI readiness gap is the #1 unmet need
85% of teachers used AI in 2024–25, yet 71% lack formal training and 85% feel unprepared to manage it. The gap isn't technology — it's confidence and workflow integration.
Insight 03
Student AI adoption has outpaced institutions
88% of students used generative AI for coursework in 2025. No major platform has an academic integrity AI layer. This is a white-space growing by the semester.
Insight 04
Blackboard's bankruptcy opens a migration window
Anthology filed Chapter 11 in Sept 2025, emerged as Blackboard Inc. in Feb 2026. ~12% of North American higher ed institutions are actively re-evaluating their stack — a narrow window Zoom must capture.
Insight 05
Tool fragmentation is a top institutional pain point
IT admins manage 16+ software tools on average. Only 19% of educators are satisfied with LMS-video integration. 43% of university CIOs plan to consolidate their EdTech stack within 2 years.

03 — Core problem statement

K-12 teachers are using Zoom as a classroom every day — but Zoom was designed for boardrooms. As AI becomes the defining differentiator in EdTech and students hit critical AI adoption mass (88%), teachers are left holding a powerful conferencing tool with no attendance tracking, no engagement visibility, no academic integrity layer, and no LMS workflow — surrounded by competitors who are rapidly closing the gap.
